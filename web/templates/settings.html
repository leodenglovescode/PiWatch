    UPS power

    Battery alerts and mains detection for your UPS.

    GPIO17 HIGH means USB-C / 5 V input is present; LOW means it is absent. The state must remain unchanged for the debounce period before an alert. Mains events include the current INA219 battery voltage and are sent independently through every enabled gateway.

    Published entities

    The publisher reads PiWatch’s public snapshot and never accesses INA219 or GPIO directly. Discovery and values are retained. Entities keep their last value through short restarts and become unavailable only after the configured timeout. The Data fresh entity separately reports whether the watchdog snapshot is current.
